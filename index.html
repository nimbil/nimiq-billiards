How to Play

Objective

Pocket all your designated balls (solids 1-7 or stripes 9-15) then legally pocket the 8-ball to win.

Break & Ball Assignment

  • The first player to legally pocket a ball after the break claims that group
  • If you pocket balls on the break, you can choose your group
  • If no ball is pocketed, the table remains open

Legal Shot

  • You must hit one of your own balls first
  • After contact, any ball must reach a cushion or be pocketed
  • Failing to do so is a foul — opponent gets ball-in-hand

Fouls

  • Scratching (cue ball pocketed) — opponent places ball anywhere
  • Hitting opponent's ball first
  • Not hitting any ball
  • Pocketing the 8-ball before clearing your group = instant loss

Controls

  • Click & Drag: Aim the cue stick — drag further for more power
  • Release: Shoot the cue ball
  • Right-click / Cancel: Reset your aim
  • Power Meter: Shows shot strength on the left

Game Modes

  • Play with AI: Practice against an AI opponent (Easy/Medium/Hard/Expert)
  • Normal: Match with real players, no NIM at stake
  • Nimiq Match: Competitive play — entry fee in NIM, winner takes the pot

Winning

  • Pocket all your balls, then legally sink the 8-ball
  • Pocketing the 8-ball in the wrong pocket or early = loss
  • In NIM matches, the winner earns NIM from the prize pool
